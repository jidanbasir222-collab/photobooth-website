❤️ For Couples Across Distance

LDR Photobooth

“Just you and me, wherever we are.”

Connect two devices (phone and laptop) in a private couple room. See and hear each other live with WebRTC, count down together in sync, and print your shared couple photostrip!

  • 🔒 Private 2-Person Room: Shareable unguessable invite link
  • 📹 Live Video & Audio: Direct peer-to-peer WebRTC streaming
  • ⏱️ Synchronized Countdown: Both cameras snap in harmony
  • 🎞️ Shared Couple Strip: Combined into one vintage PNG keepsake
👥 Besties & Squads

Friends Photobooth

“Make memories together.”

Designed for groups! Choose between gathering in front of one camera or connecting with friends in different locations.

📸 Local Mode (In One Place)

Pose together in front of one phone or laptop camera. Instant filters, stamps, and no call setup needed!

🌐 Remote Mode (Different Locations)

Friends join a private room from their own devices. Synchronized group countdown and composite squad strip!

🔒

Your Privacy is Always Protected

We believe your memories are deeply personal. Camera and microphone access runs 100% inside your browser. Live video and audio calls are direct peer-to-peer (WebRTC) and are never recorded or stored on any server. Your final photo strips are composed directly on your device using HTML5 Canvas.

Simple & Seamless

How Little Memories Works

♡

Create timeless photobooth strips in three simple steps right from your browser!

1
🚪

Choose Mode & Enter

Select LDR Couple Mode to create a private room for two, or Friends Mode (local single camera or remote group call). No account required!

💡 Tip: Share your room link via WhatsApp, iMessage, or Instagram DM.
2
📸

Strike a Pose in Sync

Watch the animated synchronized countdown on screen (3s, 5s, or 10s). Both cameras capture at the target moment. Review each shot and retake if someone blinked!

💡 Tip: Try matching poses: funny glasses, finger hearts, or mirror poses!
3
🎀

Customize & Print

Apply 8 vintage filters, pick pastel backgrounds, add cute doodles, nicknames, and date stamps. Export a high-resolution PNG ready for scrapbooking or printing!

💡 Tip: Both participants get the exact same composite photo strip!
