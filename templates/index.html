How Our Booth Works

♡

Snap your memories together in three effortless steps right from your laptop or phone camera!

1
⚙️

Set Up Your Booth

Allow camera access with a single click. Choose between 3 or 4 photo strips, set mirror mode, switch cameras, and pick your favorite countdown timer (3s, 5s, or 10s).

💡 Tip: 5-second countdown gives you just enough time to fix your hair and strike the pose!
2
📸

Strike a Pose

Watch the animated countdown on screen, hear the shutter click, and see your photo instantly. Love it? Keep it! Didn't like it? Tap retake without losing your progress.

💡 Tip: Try matching poses: funny glasses, finger hearts, or goofy surprised faces!
3
🎀

Save the Memories

Customize your strip! Pick vintage, black & white, or soft pink filters. Choose pastel backgrounds, add cute doodles and date stamps, then download a high-res PNG image.

💡 Tip: Print your downloaded strip on photo paper for an authentic vintage scrapbook feel.
